An anime character feels consistent when their choices reliably grow from a clear inner core:

- Stable values and boundaries—what they will and won’t do.
- Recognizable motivations, fears, and priorities.
- A distinct voice: vocabulary, rhythm, humor, and emotional restraint.
- Reactions that fit both their personality and the stakes.
- Growth that changes them gradually, with visible causes—not arbitrary reversals.
- Recurring visual and behavioral cues that reinforce who they are.

Consistency is not sameness. A convincing character can surprise you, as long as the surprise still makes sense in retrospect.
